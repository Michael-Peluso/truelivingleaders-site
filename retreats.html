Executive Retreats for owners and their leadership teams

Step away from the business. Decompress. Come back with the next level in focus. The Rubicon gives the CEO four days in Moab to reset and return with the vision. The Leadership Team Retreat takes your whole team off site to align and build the next quarter together.

Why step away

Some things you only see from the outside.

Inside the week, the urgent work comes first. Bids go out, jobs get built, deadlines get met. The bigger questions, like where the company goes next and what it will take to get there, need more room than a Tuesday gives them. A retreat takes you far enough out of the week to rest, see the whole company from the outside and decide what comes next, so the next quarter goes to what grows the company.

Two formats

The vision for the next level. A whole team aligned behind it.

From your application to a plan on Monday.

  1. 1 · ApplyTell us about your company and the format you want.

  2. 2 · PlanSet the goals, the people and the place.

Questions about the retreats.

Is a retreat a vacation or a working session?

Both. The first part is for decompressing. The rest is for working on the business and building the plan.

Where do the retreats happen?

The Rubicon runs in Moab, Utah, with other destinations by request. For a team retreat, we choose the destination with you.

How long is a retreat?

The Rubicon is four days. A leadership team retreat is set with you, based on what the quarter needs.

Who comes on a team retreat?

The owner and the leaders who own the lanes, with the True Living team. The people whose decisions move the scorecard are the people who come.

When are retreats scheduled?

The next set dates for The Rubicon are October 19 to 22, 2027, and other dates are available by request. Team retreats are scheduled with you on the planning call.

What if we'd rather stay at the office?

Choose an Onsite Session for that quarter. Our team comes to your company and runs the quarterly session in your building.

What does it cost?

It depends on the format, the length and the destination. We cover it on the qualifying call.
