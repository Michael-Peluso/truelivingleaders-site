About True Living

True Living is a small team of people who build and run businesses. We install True Living OS, a simple operating system with AI built in, so established companies and their leadership teams get an easier way to grow.

The Art of Freedom

What we do

We help leadership teams take established companies to the next level of growth.

We work with established companies doing $5M or more a year, and with the CEOs and leadership teams who run them. Most have outgrown the structure that built them. Nobody did anything wrong. The company grew faster than the way it makes decisions, and past a certain size too many of those decisions still route through one seat. Growth slows to match.

True Living OS is the operating system we install for the next stage. Every leader gets clear authority over their area and real-time numbers every week. Finance sees cash, margin and receivables early, every number has an owner, and the CEO's time goes to vision, key relationships and the next big move. Under the hood it's a management ecosystem: clear numbers, clear lanes, a rhythm for decisions, written processes and AI agents that do the gathering and the follow-up. We work through it as a process. Map, Design, Install and Grow, then again each time the bottleneck moves. The method inside it is the TEAM System. Time, Executives, AI and Momentum.

Construction is our featured industry. We also work with established companies outside construction, and the system is built to fit them.

What we believe

Six ideas behind the work.

  1. Fix the bottleneck first.

    Every company has one link that sets its pace. Effort anywhere else barely moves the company.

  2. Work on the business.

    The CEO's time is the company's most valuable asset. It belongs to where the company goes next.

  3. Control by design.

    Clear authority and real-time numbers let every leader make the calls in their area, and keep the whole company in control.

  4. AI should take work off people.

    The agents gather, draft and follow up. People make the decisions.

  5. A company should pay you twice.

    Once in time back, and once in what it's worth.

  6. Be adventurous.

    It's the last of the True Living agreements, and the easiest one to forget.

About the Founder

Michael Peluso

Founder, True Living · Investor and entrepreneur

Michael Peluso is the founder of True Living. He grew up in a family of business owners, and today he is an investor and entrepreneur who owns and operates construction businesses.

  • Advised, and held equity in, a residential concrete company that grew more than sevenfold, from one subcontractor to six salespeople, an office team, its own building and three locations.
  • Worked with an owner whose CFO forecast 10 years before he could step back. With his leadership team running the day-to-day, he stepped back years ahead of that forecast, and later moved into land development.
  • Trained athletes for years, and has worked with top-performing national and international athletes and entrepreneurs.
  • Nearly two decades in human performance and leadership.
  • Has spoken on stage at investor masterminds, including the Investor Expo in Las Vegas in 2021, and has hosted masterminds and retreats across the US, from Disney and the Ozarks to Vermont and Florida.

Michael built the original TEAM method for high performers, around how they manage Time, Energy, Agreements and Money. Running construction businesses showed him the same levers at company scale. A company moves at the speed of its slowest link, and past $5M that link is usually the way decisions are structured, not the people making them. True Living OS draws on both. Today he leads the True Living team and hosts The Rubicon, our Executive Retreat in Moab.

Away from work, Michael lives in Warren County, New Jersey, with his wife and kids. Family, time outdoors and a little adventure are the reasons he does the work. They're also the reasons he wants every owner and every leader to have more time of their own.
