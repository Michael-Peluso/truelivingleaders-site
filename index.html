The next stage

Information doesn't change how a company runs. People do.

Most programs, and now AI, hand owners more information: frameworks, dashboards, summaries. None of it changes how a CEO leads or how a leadership team works together. That's the work we do, with you and with your leaders, inside your company.

Where growth leaks

It's not only volume. Growth leaks in three places.

Most programs look at one thing: how much work the company can take on. We look at all three, and start with whichever is costing your company the most.

Volume

The work the company can carry.

Bids, starts and crews held back by how fast decisions get made, not by demand.

Profit

The margin between bid and closeout.

Change orders billed late, estimates that drift from actuals and pricing calls that wait. Small leaks on every job add up.

Time

The hours your leaders spend waiting.

Your week and your leaders' weeks, spent gathering information, waiting on approvals and redoing work.

Check for yourself

Three signs your company is ready for its next structure.

If these sound familiar, the company has likely outgrown the structure that built it. The Growth Check asks six questions, one for each part of the company, and shows which part sets the pace today. It takes about two minutes.

How we work

We work with you, and with the team that carries it.

The CEO

From running the work to leading the change.

Every week you bring what's on your mind: the hire you're weighing, the seat that needs to grow, the big decision, the next move. We work through it with you, starting from where the company is going and working backward to the numbers, the seats and the decisions it takes to get there.

We've seen what happens next. When roles and standards get clear, some seats change. We plan for that with you before it becomes an issue, so the company keeps moving.

The leadership team

A team that builds the process and owns it.

Your leaders build their processes together, so problems get solved instead of passed around. Each leader owns their area, their numbers and their decisions, with the authority to make the call.

As the team brings AI in, their time moves from gathering information to the work that grows the company: decisions, clients and their people.

Work with only the CEO and nothing changes on Tuesday. Install systems without the CEO and they fade. Doing both is what makes it stick.

Your time

Your week goes back to growing the company.

The CEO's time is the company's most valuable asset. As your leaders take on more of the day-to-day, your hours go back to vision, key relationships and the next venture, and the company keeps growing while it happens.

Questions CEOs ask before they apply.

How is this different from a program or a peer group?

Programs and peer groups hand you information and ideas. We work inside your company: with you on real decisions every week, and with your leaders as they build the processes and own them.

What if someone doesn't meet the new standard?

As roles and standards get clear, most leaders rise to them. Where a seat needs someone new, we see it coming and plan it with you before it becomes an issue, so the company keeps moving and the next hire goes into a clear role.

What does it change for our CFO and finance team?

The numbers are built from the books you already close. Cash, margin and receivables get seen early, and spending and pricing limits are set each quarter, so finance spends its time steering the company instead of gathering reports.

What's the first step?

The Growth Map, a defined first step with a clear finish line. We map the company with you and your leaders and build a baseline from your real numbers, showing where volume, profit and time are leaking. The company keeps it.

How much of my time does it take?

A weekly session with Michael, your part in the weekly leadership rhythm and a quarterly session on site. The point is to put more of your week into what grows the company.

What does the AI do?

It takes over the gathering and reporting, so your leaders' time goes to decisions, clients and their people. You choose what to connect, and people make the decisions.

Do you only work with construction companies?

Yes. We work with construction companies doing $5M or more: commercial GCs, specialty contractors and residential contractors.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of your leadership team and how you want to run the quarterly sessions. We cover it on the qualifying call, once we know what your company needs.
