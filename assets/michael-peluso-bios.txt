Michael Peluso is the founder of True Living. His team installs True Living OS, a simple operating system with AI built in, in established companies doing $5M or more. The work starts with the Theory of Constraints. Every company has one link that sets its pace. Past $5M, it is usually how decisions are structured, not who makes them. True Living OS clears it with clear numbers, leaders who own their areas, a rhythm for decisions, written processes and AI agents that gather and follow up. Michael is an investor and entrepreneur who owns and operates construction businesses. He advised, and held equity in, a residential concrete company that grew more than sevenfold, to three locations with a full sales and office team. He trained athletes for years, has spent nearly two decades in human performance and leadership, and has spoken at investor masterminds, including the Investor Expo in Las Vegas.
