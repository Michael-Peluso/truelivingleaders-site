Signature talk

The Slowest Link

Finding the link that sets a growing company's pace, and building the structure that takes it to the next level

Owners and leadership teams leave this talk knowing what sets their company's pace and what to fix first. Every company has one link that sets its pace. Past $5M, it's usually the way decisions are structured. The company has grown faster than the way it decides, and too many calls still route through one seat. Michael Peluso shows how to find that link with Eliyahu Goldratt's Theory of Constraints, and how to clear it with a management ecosystem. Clear numbers, leaders who own their areas and their decisions, a rhythm for decisions, written processes and AI agents that gather and follow up. One owner's CFO said it would take ten years before he could step back. He was out of the day-to-day years ahead of that forecast, and used the time to set a bigger goal.

Best for: owners, CEOs, CFOs, controllers and leadership teams of established companies doing $5M or more a year. Construction audiences get construction examples.

Three takeaways

  1. Find your bottleneck.A two-week decision log and one number, executive lag, show where decisions slow down and how fast the company really decides.
  2. Build the ecosystem around it.The scorecard, the lanes, the decision rights and a rhythm that frees the CEO's time for vision and the next big move.
  3. Put AI agents to work.Agents that gather the numbers, draft SOPs and follow up, so execution gets faster and more consistent.

Built for the person who keeps the numbers. Finance leaders leave with a plan for real-time numbers every week, every number owned, and cash, margin and receivables seen early.
