Who we partner with

Good companies, run by people we trust.

A partnership is a long relationship, so we're selective. Here's what we look for.

  • Founders we believe in.People who do what they say, take care of their teams and want to build something that lasts.
  • A real business.Customers, revenue and numbers you're willing to open up. Earlier-stage companies with real traction are welcome to reach out too.
  • A clear next stage.A new market, a second location, a new product, or a leadership team ready to run more of the company so the CEO's time goes to the next big move.
  • Room for our team to help.Companies where our operating experience, True Living OS and our network can make a real difference. Any industry. Construction is where our roots run deepest.

What partnership can look like

Four ways to work together.

Every partnership is built around the company and the people in it. These are the usual starting points.

  • Equity partnership

    We take an ownership stake and work alongside you to grow the company.

  • Joint venture

    We build something new together, such as a new service, market or company, with shared ownership.

  • Strategic investment

    We put capital into your company, along with our team's experience, where both can help the next stage happen.

  • Something else

    Some of the best partnerships don't fit a label. Tell us what you have in mind.

How it works

Inquiry. Conversation. Fit.

  1. Inquiry.

    Tell us about your company and the kind of partnership you have in mind. It takes about five minutes.

  2. Conversation.

    If it looks promising, we set up a call with Michael and our team to learn the business and the people behind it.

  3. Fit.

    We move forward only when both sides see a clear fit. If it isn't one, we'll tell you plainly.

Every partnership is structured on its own terms, with its own agreements and with advisors on both sides.
