The True Living annual mastermind · 2027

Ridgeline, the off-road mastermind for contractors doing $5M+.

A small group of contractor owners. Days on the trail. Working sessions on the business, where each owner finds the bottleneck in their company and works it with the group. 2027, dates announced soon. Applications are open now.

Apply for Ridgeline 2027 See how it works

By invitation, after an application and a call.

The name

From the ridgeline, you see the whole map.

Down in the valley, you see the next turn. Up on the ridgeline, you see where you've been, where the trail goes and where it gets hard. That's what working on the business means. You step out of the week, look at the whole company from above and choose your line before you drive it. Ridgeline is time set aside to do that, with owners on the same climb.

How it works

On the trail, in the room and around the table.

  1. 01

    On the trail

    Off-road days, far from the office and the week. Hard lines, honest conversation, and the clear head that only comes from distance.

  2. 02

    In the room

    Working sessions on the business. Each owner brings their numbers and the place where decisions take longest. The group finds the bottleneck in each company, one company at a time, and works out what to do about it. The frame is the one our team uses with clients. Find the bottleneck, fix it first, then find the next one.

  3. 03

    Around the table

    Unhurried time with owners who carry the same weight you do.

The group

Every hard line needs a spotter.

On a steep obstacle, the driver can't see the tires. The spotter can. They stand outside the vehicle and call the line. A small room of owners does the same for each other. Each one sees what the others can't see from the driver's seat, and that includes the bottleneck an owner stops noticing after years of driving around it.

Seats are limited so every owner gets real time in the room.

What you leave with

You go home with the next climb mapped.

  • Your company's bottleneck, named and worked through by owners who've been there.
  • Your next quarter's plan, built in the room and tested by the group.
  • A peer group of contractor owners at your level.
  • Days off the grid that remind you why you built the company.

Who it's for

Built for contractor owners who are ready to shift gears.

  • You own a construction or trade contracting company doing $5M or more a year.
  • You have leaders in place, and you want to spend more of your time on the business.
  • You'll bring real numbers and real problems into the room.
  • You're up for some adventure.

Questions about Ridgeline.

When is Ridgeline 2027?

Dates will be announced soon. Applications are open now, and applicants hear the dates first.

Where is it?

We'll announce the location with the dates.

Is it only for construction?

Yes. Ridgeline is for owners of construction and trade contracting companies doing $5M or more a year. Owners in other industries can apply for True Living OS or an Executive Retreat.

Do I need off-road experience?

We'll share what's needed with the dates. The application asks about your off-road experience and your vehicle, so we can plan around you.

Can I bring a partner or a key leader?

Ridgeline is built for owners. If you'd like to bring someone, raise it on the qualifying call.

How is this different from The Rubicon?

Ridgeline is a small group of contractor owners working on their companies together. The Rubicon is four days in Moab's canyon country, built around one company, to reset, see the business from the outside and come back with the vision for the next level.

What does it cost?

We cover the cost on the qualifying call.
