The Growth Check

Every company has one link that sets its pace. Past $5M, it's very often the way decisions are structured, with too many still routing through one seat. Answer six questions about the last month, one for each part of your company. You'll see where decisions route to you most, and where clear authority would speed the company up.

    What happens next

    Four steps, and you decide at every one.

    1. Apply.

      Answer the questions below. Be as specific as you can. The more we know, the better the call.

    2. Book your qualifying call.

      As soon as you submit, you'll pick a time. The call is 30 minutes with our team.

    3. The call.

      We look at where the slowest link sits in your company, what would free your time and give your leaders more room, what True Living OS would clear first, and whether we're a fit. If we're not, we'll tell you.

    4. Your plan.

      If it's a fit, we send a proposal with the scope, the approach and the price, and we set your start date.
